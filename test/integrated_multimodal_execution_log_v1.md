## Why this experiment was run

Earlier experiments evaluated news fusion and regime/SHAP routing in separate
modules. This run closes that forecasting-integration gap by feeding the frozen
eight-feature Local NLP news block into the frozen five-model regime-SHAP
pipeline on one common 2019-start cohort. It does not turn the partial-2026
Track D test into a news-integrated test because no frozen 2026 news feature
file exists.

The downstream news features were generated by the expanding/out-of-sample
Local NLP pipeline. The LLM single-pass and Bull/Bear/Leader debate results
remain a separate intrinsic benchmark. No LLM output was represented as the
source of these downstream features.

Models and frozen windows: LSTM W5, CNN W20, LSTM-CNN W20,
LSTM-Attention W10, and LSTM-CNN-Attention W20. Seeds were 42, 123,

Every arm used the same 2019-start training cohort, target dates, train-only
scaling, sequence context, and point-in-time label purge.

Interpretation:

- Global news effect was negative in all five architectures, although CNN was
  effectively unchanged at -0.009 pp.
- Within the regime-SHAP pipeline, news improved BAcc in 2/5 models (LSTM and
  LSTM-Attention) and reduced it in 3/5.
- The final integrated arm exceeded Global-Numeric in 2/5 models (LSTM and
  LSTM-CNN-Attention), but not consistently by fold.
- The highest mean BAcc in the full 2 x 2 table was 54.072% from
  LSTM-CNN Regime-SHAP-Numeric, not a news arm.

## Multiplicity and temporal uncertainty

No BAcc contrast passed Holm adjustment across the five architectures. Every
Holm-adjusted fold-level BAcc p-value was 1.000 except the
LSTM-Attention routing-news interaction, which was 0.625 (raw exact sign-flip
p=0.125). No BAcc moving-block-bootstrap contrast passed Holm adjustment.

The LSTM-Attention interaction was positive in all four folds (+2.929 pp), but
its bootstrap 95% interval crossed zero [-0.712, 6.557] pp and its adjusted
bootstrap p-value was 0.543. It is therefore a hypothesis for future data, not
evidence of a confirmed interaction.

## Regression/direction trade-off

The news block sometimes improved level-forecast RMSE while weakening BAcc.
For example, Global-Numeric-News reduced mean RMSE for LSTM-Attention by
8.069 points but reduced BAcc by 1.895 pp. In the final integrated comparison,
LSTM-Attention reduced RMSE by 10.447 points versus Global-Numeric while BAcc
fell by 0.994 pp. Directional and level-forecast claims must therefore remain
separate.

## Runtime record and anomaly

Raw cell-wall total was 9,721.258 seconds. Median cell wall time was 54.957
seconds. Model-level median cell times were LSTM 31.528 s, CNN 33.981 s,
LSTM-CNN 58.551 s, LSTM-Attention 56.849 s, and LSTM-CNN-Attention 80.270 s.

One raw timing observation is an extreme system/runtime outlier:
LSTM-CNN-Attention, fold 1, base seed 456, Regime-SHAP-Numeric Bull fit took
4,447.497 seconds; its cell wall time was 4,605.171 seconds. The cell produced
finite aligned predictions and passed integrity checks. It was not removed or
rerun. Runtime reporting should show medians and retain the raw outlier in the
supplement.

## Claim decision

This run completes the planned 2019--2025 news + regime-SHAP forecasting
integration, but it does not support a claim that news improves next-day SET50
direction prediction. Its defensible contribution is a leakage-audited,
budget-aware, negative/mixed-result multimodal reliability experiment.
