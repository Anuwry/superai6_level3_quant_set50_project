# Citation audit — 11 August 2026

## Scope and policy

The manuscript was audited at the claim-to-source level. Foundational papers
were retained when they define the method being used; recency was not used to
replace the original LSTM, attention, VMD, SHAP, LIME, regime, or statistical
sources. Recent literature was added where the manuscript previously lacked a
current account of financial backtesting, decomposition, multimodal news,
financial LLM agents, explainability, or uncertainty.

Conference papers were retained when they are the primary archival source from
a major computing venue, including NeurIPS, IJCAI, ICML, ACL, EMNLP, AAAI, and
KDD. Journal quartiles were not used as a substitute for topical relevance or
primary-source status.

## Placement changes

1. The five-item architecture cluster in the Introduction was split by claim:
   foundational architecture papers now support model definitions, while
   Fischer and Krauss (2018) and Hoseinzade and Haratizadeh (2019) support
   representative financial applications.
2. Current reviews now support claims about reproducibility, backtesting, and
   practical evaluation instead of leaving those claims attached only to older
   architecture papers.
3. VMD, multimodal-news, financial-agent, financial-XAI, uncertainty, circular
   bootstrap, and SET-index-methodology claims now have direct sources.
4. The Data Availability section now cites the two exact historical-data pages
   rather than relying only on the earlier Materials section.
5. The Holm record was corrected from a non-resolving DOI field to its JSTOR
   stable URL.

## Working-style and Zotero status

The citation-complete Word draft uses APA 7 as a neutral working style because
the submission journal is not yet selected. The citations and bibliography in
that DOCX were generated by Pandoc citeproc and are formatted Word text, not
live Zotero fields. This is suitable for reading and review. Once the target
journal is selected, the document must either be regenerated with the journal's
CSL file or migrated to Zotero fields before using Zotero's global style switch.

The source of record for future citation regeneration is the combination of
`manuscript_v1.md` and `references.bib`.
